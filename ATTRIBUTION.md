# Attribution

## Scoring keys and weights

`Distilled Key.csv` and `Optimized_Keys.csv` are derived from the research
materials of:

> Samo, A., Garrido, L. E., Abad, F. J., Golino, H., McAbee, S. T., &
> Christensen, A. P. (2026). Revisiting the IPIP-NEO personality hierarchy
> with taxonomic graph analysis. *European Journal of Personality*, 40(2),
> 369–390. https://doi.org/10.1177/08902070251352590

The materials are published on the Open Science Framework
(https://osf.io/hwpa9) under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The keys here were adapted (distilled and reordered for adaptive
administration). Keep this notice with any copy of these files.

## Questionnaire items

The items are from the International Personality Item Pool
(https://ipip.ori.org), which is in the public domain.
